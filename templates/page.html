Laning phase

Trades and kill pressure

Bot lane is won with short trades, not 100-to-0 fights. Each trade is about 2 seconds of autos and Q into the enemy's retaliation, then Rend as Kalista backs off. The score is the net HP lead it creates.

Quick is a ~2 s trade and Extended a ~3.5 s trade, both as enemy % max HP lost minus Kalista's % max HP lost. Kill pressure is the highest enemy HP % she can kill within 2 seconds while at 80% HP herself. Execute is the time to finish a 25% HP target from 60% HP before it escapes.

How the duel works, and its limits

  • Both sides start at full HP and fight to the death. Kalista casts Q on cooldown, stacks spears and fires Rend when it kills. Enemies auto-attack and use an opener ability with crowd control, then repeat it on cooldown.
  • Kalista heals from lifesteal, omnivamp, Conqueror and Fleet Footwork, and is protected by Shieldbow, Maw, Bloodthirster's shield, Death's Dance and Guardian Angel. Grievous Wounds cut her healing by 40% once enemies have their wounds item. Tenacity shortens crowd control.
  • Melee enemies land only part of their damage because Kalista hops while attacking (the kiting check shows how much this matters).
  • Guardian Angel is a second life in a 1v1, so the plain duel goal overvalues it. Its 300-second cooldown and teamfights make it much weaker in real games, which is why the no-GA duel build is shown separately.
  • Not modeled: positioning, skillshots missing, teammates, summoner spells, mana, and enemy items beyond the profile. Treat scores as relative between builds, not as real win rates.
